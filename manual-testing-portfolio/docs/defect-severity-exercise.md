# Defect Severity Exercise

Practice in classifying defects by severity for everyday objects. These are illustrative examples, not defects found in a real product.

## 1. Digital watch

| Severity | Defect |
|---|---|
| Blocker | Watch does not work even after putting in a new cell |
| Critical | Watch works but skips 15 minutes |
| Major | Watch does not make the beep sound every hour |
| Minor | Spelling mistake in the name on the watch; cut-out of the front is uneven |

## 2. Pen drive

| Severity | Defect |
|---|---|
| Blocker | Cannot be inserted because of a design issue; PC cannot detect the pen drive after inserting |
| Critical | After inserting, it cannot load all the files |
| Major | Transfer speed is very slow; file details do not load |
| Minor | Brand name is not shown after inserting; spelling mistakes printed on the pen drive |

## 3. A4 paper

| Severity | Defect |
|---|---|
| Blocker | Paper is black on both sides; size is not A4 |
| Critical | Writing gets erased when rubbed; ink spreads on the paper |
| Major | Small dots on the paper; paper is very thin |
| Minor | A fold in a corner |
